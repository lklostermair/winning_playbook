# Scope of License and Exclusivity

## Standard Position
Grant of a worldwide, perpetual, and exclusive right to use the IP/Trademark for all commercial purposes. The Licensor must explicitly waive any retained right to use or sublicense the IP in the licensed field.

## Fallback Position
- Grant of a 'sole license' where the Licensor retains the right to use the IP only for non-competing internal operations but is contractually barred from granting further licenses to third-party competitors.

## Red Line
Non-exclusive license status while the startup is still expected to pay a premium for 'exclusive' market access, providing no barrier to entry against direct market challengers.

## Rationale
Exclusivity defines the competitive moat for the startup. VCs require absolute clarity here to ensure that the licensed IP cannot be undermined by the Licensor or competitors within the same geographic and industry verticals, effectively protecting the future value of the asset.

## Negotiation Tips
- Ambiguous wording that implies 'exclusivity' but includes 'reserved rights' for the Licensor to continue selling directly or licensing to third parties, which dilutes market control.
