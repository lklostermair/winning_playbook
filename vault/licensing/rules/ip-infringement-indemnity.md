# IP Infringement Indemnity

## Standard Position
Full indemnity and legal defense provided by the Licensor for any third-party claims alleging that the licensed IP infringes on their intellectual property rights, including the payment of all legal fees and resulting damages.

## Fallback Position
- Indemnity coverage is provided but capped at the total amount of royalties paid under the agreement during the preceding 24 months, balancing risk between the two parties.

## Red Line
The startup is required to indemnify the Licensor for claims arising from the Licensor’s own failure to secure valid IP rights, effectively shifting the burden of the Licensor’s legal failures onto the startup.

## Rationale
The startup must be protected against claims that the licensed IP itself infringes on third-party rights. Without this, the startup risks building its entire product on a foundation that could be sued out of existence by a competitor.

## Negotiation Tips
- Clauses that provide the IP 'as is' or specifically disclaim any warranty of title, which effectively forces the startup to bear the full risk of the Licensor’s IP being legally invalid.
