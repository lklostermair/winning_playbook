# Principal and Maturity

## Standard Position
A fixed term of 3-5 years with a clearly defined 'bullet' repayment or an amortization schedule that aligns perfectly with the startup’s projected cash flow cycles.

## Fallback Position
- A term linked to a specific, identifiable event, such as the closing of a Series B funding round, with a 'long-stop' date of at least 24 months.

## Red Line
No fixed maturity date, or a maturity date shorter than the current runway, which creates immediate and unmanageable insolvency risk.

## Rationale
Sets the total debt exposure. A proper maturity date ensures the company reaches the next 'value inflection point' before principal repayment becomes mandatory.

## Negotiation Tips
- Demand loans where the lender can demand immediate repayment at any time, which creates an uncontrollable risk for the company’s ongoing viability.
