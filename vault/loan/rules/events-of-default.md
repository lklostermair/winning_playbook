# Events of Default

## Standard Position
Defaults limited to non-payment of principal/interest and formal insolvency. A 'cure period' of 15 business days is mandatory for all other technical breaches.

## Fallback Position
- Events of default include the breach of financial covenants, but only if the breach is not remedied within a generous 30-day grace period.

## Red Line
Immediate acceleration of the full loan balance for any 'material adverse change' in company prospects, judged solely by the lender.

## Rationale
Defines the triggers for acceleration, where the full loan becomes due. Must be limited to avoid catastrophic defaults for minor technical errors.

## Negotiation Tips
- Cross-default clauses where a breach of a minor, unrelated contract (like a office equipment lease) triggers an automatic default on the entire loan.
