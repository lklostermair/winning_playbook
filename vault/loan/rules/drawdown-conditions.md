# Drawdown Conditions

## Standard Position
Drawdown is subject only to clear, objective requirements: signed board resolutions, proof of corporate insurance, and a valid legal opinion on corporate capacity.

## Fallback Position
- Drawdown linked to achieving specific, measurable financial milestones, such as reaching a set monthly recurring revenue (MRR) or completing a product launch phase.

## Red Line
Lender has absolute, unmediated discretion to refuse funding for any reason, even if the startup has fully complied with all contractual obligations.

## Rationale
Defines the hurdles to actually receive the cash. Startups must ensure these are objective so the lender cannot arbitrarily back out of the agreement.

## Negotiation Tips
- Subjective 'Material Adverse Change' (MAC) clauses or conditions based on the lender’s 'sole satisfaction,' which allow the lender to escape the deal if the market shifts.
