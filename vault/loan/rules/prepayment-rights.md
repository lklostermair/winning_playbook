# Prepayment Rights

## Standard Position
The right to prepay the loan at any time, in whole or in part, with no penalty, subject only to a short 30-day notice period.

## Fallback Position
- Prepayment permitted subject to a minimal 'break fee' (e.g., 1-2% of the principal) to compensate the lender for the administrative cost of the exit.

## Red Line
A 'Lock-out' period of several years during which prepayment is strictly forbidden, as this blocks potential M&A exits or debt restructuring.

## Rationale
Crucial for an exit. If a startup is acquired or raises a massive round, it should be able to pay off debt without paying 'make-whole' penalties.

## Negotiation Tips
- Total prohibition on early prepayment or 'make-whole' provisions that force the startup to pay all interest that *would* have been due until the end of the term.
