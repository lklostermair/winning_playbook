# Interest Rates

## Standard Position
A fixed interest rate for the duration of the loan, or a floating rate (EURIBOR/SOFR) with a clear, enforceable cap to prevent rising costs.

## Fallback Position
- Floating rate with no ceiling, but accompanied by the explicit right for the company to prepay the loan without penalty if the rate exceeds a certain threshold.

## Red Line
Uncapped floating rates that automatically adjust upwards based on subjective company performance metrics or external macro-fluctuations.

## Rationale
The cost of capital. For early-stage companies, interest should ideally be 'PIK' (Paid-in-Kind) to preserve cash while the startup is scaling.

## Negotiation Tips
- Floating rates without a clear ceiling, or 'default interest' rates that are punitively high (e.g., 15% above the base rate) upon any minor breach.
