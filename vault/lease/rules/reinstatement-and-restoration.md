# Reinstatement and Restoration

## Standard Position
Tenant is not required to remove standard office improvements (glass walls, cabling, lighting) at the end of the term, provided they were installed with consent.

## Fallback Position
- Tenant only removes 'highly unusual' installations (e.g., lab fume hoods) while leaving standard partitions and office infrastructure.

## Red Line
Mandatory full professional renovation of the space regardless of its actual condition upon exit.

## Rationale
Exit costs can be enormous. Removing custom meeting rooms and glass walls can cost thousands. The lease must protect the startup from these unnecessary costs.

## Negotiation Tips
- Requirements to return the space to 'shell and core' condition even if the landlord originally approved the improvements.
