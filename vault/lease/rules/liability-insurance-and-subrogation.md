# Liability, Insurance, and Subrogation

## Standard Position
Liability is capped at insurance limits; mutual waiver of subrogation (insurance companies can't sue after paying claims).

## Fallback Position
- Tenant only liable for repairs exceeding normal wear and tear.

## Red Line
Accepting liability for Landlord's 'loss of profit' or business interruption if the building is damaged.

## Decision Logic
_Not specified._

## Escalation Logic
Escalate if Landlord insists on tenant liability for Landlord's loss of profit/business interruption or rejects mutual waiver of subrogation.

## Rationale
Protects against building failure and indirect losses. Essential that insurance companies don't sue the startup for building-wide events.

## Negotiation Tips
- Clauses requiring the startup to indemnify the Landlord even for the Landlord’s own negligence.
