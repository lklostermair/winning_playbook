# Permitted Use and Operational Flexibility

## Standard Position
Premises may be used for 'General office, administrative, and technological business purposes, including associated R&D and storage.'

## Fallback Position
- Specific use is defined, but the Landlord cannot unreasonably withhold consent for a change in use within the broader tech/service sector.

## Red Line
Clause that triggers rent increases or lease termination automatically if the startup changes its primary business focus.

## Rationale
Startups evolve rapidly; office needs change from R&D to showrooms to studios. The lease must not stifle this, or it becomes a liability for growth.

## Negotiation Tips
- Restrictive use clauses like 'Only for the purpose of a law firm' or 'Software coding only,' which kill the startup's ability to pivot its business model.
