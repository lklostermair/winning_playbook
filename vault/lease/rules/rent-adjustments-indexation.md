# Rent Adjustments (Indexation)

## Standard Position
Rent is fixed for the first 3 years. Thereafter, adjustments occur only if the CPI changes by more than 10%, with a strict cap on the maximum annual increase (e.g., 3%).

## Fallback Position
- Standard 'Step-Up' rent schedule where increases are known in advance, allowing for accurate financial planning throughout the lease term.

## Red Line
Unlimited annual indexation that occurs even if the market rate for office space in the area is stagnant or falling.

## Rationale
Inflation can destroy startup runways. Rent increases must be predictable and capped to ensure financial stability.

## Negotiation Tips
- Clauses that increase rent annually by 'CPI + X%' with no 'floor' or maximum annual limit.
