# Maintenance (Dach und Fach)

## Standard Position
Landlord responsible for structural repairs ('Dach und Fach'), exterior, and common areas. Tenant only responsible for internal, non-structural repairs up to a small annual cap (e.g., €1,000).

## Fallback Position
- Tenant pays for a pro-rata share of maintenance via service charges, but these charges are explicitly capped at a fixed percentage of the base rent.

## Red Line
Tenant is made responsible for the full replacement of major HVAC or structural systems (a 'Triple Net' lease in a decaying building).

## Rationale
Capex costs can be 'hidden rent.' Startups should not be responsible for fixing a building's roof or elevator, as these are landlord responsibilities.

## Negotiation Tips
- Clauses that make the tenant responsible for 'all repairs and replacements,' effectively shifting the risk of an aging building structure to the startup.
