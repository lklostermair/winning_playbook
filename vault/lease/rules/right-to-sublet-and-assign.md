# Right to Sublet and Assign

## Standard Position
Tenant has the right to sublet part or all of the premises to any affiliated company without consent, and to third parties with Landlord consent (not to be unreasonably withheld).

## Fallback Position
- Subletting is permitted to third parties provided the Tenant remains fully liable for the rent payments.

## Red Line
Landlord has the right to 'recapture' the space (terminate the lease) if the Tenant asks for permission to sublet.

## Rationale
The ultimate 'escape hatch.' Startups raising huge rounds need to move; those facing cutbacks need to sublet. This flexibility is vital for survival.

## Negotiation Tips
- Prohibitions on subletting even to parent or subsidiary companies.
