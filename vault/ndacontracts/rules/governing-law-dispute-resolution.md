# Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

## Standard Position
The agreement specifies a governing law (e.g., Switzerland, England and Wales) and a jurisdiction for dispute resolution (e.g., Zurich, London). Both allow for seeking injunctive or other equitable relief from any court of competent jurisdiction.

## Fallback Position
- Negotiate between 'non-exclusive' (A5) and 'exclusive' (A4) jurisdiction for dispute resolution.
- Include 'without regard to its conflict of laws principles' to ensure clarity on the applicable law.
- Explicitly exclude the United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods (CISG) (A5).
- Specify confidentiality of court proceedings and filings (A4).

## Red Line
Governing law or jurisdiction in a highly unfavorable, unpredictable, or politically unstable legal system.

## Decision Logic
For ABC (a German company), a neutral and well-understood jurisdiction like Switzerland or England and Wales is common. Exclusive jurisdiction provides more certainty and predictability for litigation, while non-exclusive jurisdiction offers flexibility to sue in other competent courts if advantageous.

## Escalation Logic
Escalate if the counterparty insists on a governing law or jurisdiction that is highly disadvantageous or unfamiliar without good reason.

## Rationale
Both contracts choose reputable European jurisdictions. The choice between exclusive and non-exclusive jurisdiction is a key negotiation point, impacting litigation strategy. A5 excludes CISG, which is common in international commercial contracts.

## Negotiation Tips
- Aim for a neutral and well-understood legal system (e.g., Switzerland, England and Wales, New York, Delaware).
- Consider the pros and cons of 'exclusive' versus 'non-exclusive' jurisdiction based on the potential for disputes and strategic litigation options.
- Always include the carve-out for injunctive relief, as it's crucial for protecting confidential information.

## Suggested Language
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of [Switzerland/England and Wales], without regard to its conflict of laws principles [and excluding the United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods (CISG)]. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be submitted to the [non-exclusive/exclusive] jurisdiction of the courts of [Zurich, Switzerland/London, England]. [All court proceedings and filings related to disputes under this Agreement shall be treated as confidential to the extent permitted by applicable law and court rules.] Nothing in this clause prevents either Party from seeking injunctive or other equitable relief from any court of competent jurisdiction.
