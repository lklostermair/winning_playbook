# Non-Solicitation

## Standard Position
During the term of the agreement and for a specified period post-termination, neither Party shall directly solicit for employment any employee of the other Party who was personally involved in the exchange of Confidential Information. Standard exceptions include general recruitment advertising and employees responding to such advertising without direct solicitation.

## Fallback Position
- Post-term duration: 6 months (A5) vs. 12 months (A4).
- Add an exception for employees who 'independently approach' the other Party seeking employment (A4).
- Explicitly state that 'No contractual penalty shall apply for breach of this clause' (A4).

## Red Line
An indefinite non-solicitation period. A clause that prevents general recruitment advertising or hiring employees who independently apply.

## Decision Logic
A longer non-solicitation period (12 months) is generally more protective for the Disclosing Party. The 'independently approaches' exception is common and reasonable to include. Explicitly excluding a contractual penalty for this clause can be a good negotiation point for the Receiving Party.

## Escalation Logic
Escalate if the counterparty proposes an overly broad non-solicitation clause that restricts general hiring practices, or an unreasonable duration (e.g., more than 12-24 months).

## Rationale
Both contracts include non-solicitation, but differ on duration (6 vs. 12 months) and specific exceptions. A4 explicitly excludes a contractual penalty for this clause, which is a meaningful variation.

## Negotiation Tips
- Aim for a 12-month post-term non-solicitation period if possible.
- Always ensure standard exceptions (general advertising, independent approach) are clearly included to protect legitimate hiring practices.
- Consider explicitly stating that no contractual penalty applies for breach of this clause, as it can be difficult to prove damages for non-solicitation.

## Suggested Language
During the term of this Agreement and for a period of [six (6) months/twelve (12) months] following its termination or expiration, neither Party shall directly solicit for employment any employee of the other Party who was personally involved in the exchange of Confidential Information under this Agreement. This restriction does not apply to: (a) general recruitment advertising or job postings; (b) any employee who responds to such advertising without direct solicitation; or (c) any employee who independently approaches the other Party seeking employment. [No contractual penalty shall apply for breach of this clause.]
