# Compelled Disclosure & Intellectual Property Rights

## Standard Position
In case of legally compelled disclosure, the Receiving Party must promptly notify the Disclosing Party (if legally permitted) and cooperate to limit the scope. Regarding Intellectual Property (IP), each Party retains its pre-existing IP, and disclosure does not grant any licenses or transfer ownership.

## Fallback Position
- Specify 'commercially reasonable efforts to obtain a protective order' for compelled disclosure.
- Address potential joint IP creation (joint ownership, negotiate terms later) if the Purpose involves collaborative development.
- Clarify ownership of derivative materials (derivative materials containing CI are CI of Disclosing Party, no implied IP assignment) if the Receiving Party will primarily analyze existing CI.

## Red Line
Any clause that implies automatic transfer of IP rights upon disclosure or allows compelled disclosure without notification/cooperation when legally possible.

## Decision Logic
The choice between a joint IP clause (A5) and a derivative materials clause (A4) depends on the specific nature of the business opportunity. If the collaboration might lead to new inventions, the joint IP clause is relevant. If it's more about analysis of existing CI, the derivative materials clause is appropriate.

## Escalation Logic
Escalate if the counterparty demands IP rights to the Disclosing Party's existing IP or refuses to cooperate on compelled disclosure.

## Rationale
Both contracts cover compelled disclosure similarly. IP clauses differ significantly, reflecting different potential collaboration models. A5 contemplates joint IP creation, while A4 focuses on derivative works and clarifies their status.

## Negotiation Tips
- Understand the nature of the collaboration to determine the most suitable IP clause.
- Always ensure the Disclosing Party's core IP is protected and that no unintended licenses or transfers are created by the NDA.

## Suggested Language
(Compelled Disclosure) 'If the Receiving Party is required by law, regulation, or legal process to disclose Confidential Information, it shall promptly notify the Disclosing Party (to the extent legally permitted) and shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain a protective order or other appropriate remedy limiting the scope of such disclosure.' (IP) 'Each Party retains all rights, title, and interest in its pre-existing intellectual property. Nothing in this Agreement grants either Party any license, right, or interest in the other Party's intellectual property. Disclosure does not constitute a transfer of ownership. [OPTIONAL: To the extent that any joint inventions or joint intellectual property arise from activities conducted under the Purpose, such joint intellectual property shall be jointly owned by the Parties, and the Parties agree to negotiate the terms of use and commercialization in a separate written agreement in good faith. / OPTIONAL: Any analyses, compilations, studies, or other derivative materials prepared by the Receiving Party that contain or are based upon Confidential Information shall be treated as Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party for the purposes of this Agreement, but no assignment of intellectual property rights in such derivative materials shall be implied.]'
