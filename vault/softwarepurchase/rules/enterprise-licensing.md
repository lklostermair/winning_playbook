# Enterprise Licensing

## Standard Position
Broad 'Enterprise License' allowing use by all employees/affiliates for internal business purposes, allowing for growth within the enterprise without per-seat renegotiation barriers.

## Fallback Position
- A 'Named User' model with a pre-negotiated, transparent price list for adding additional users or modular features as the customer scales.

## Red Line
Technical restrictions on virtualized/cloud environments (e.g., Citrix or Azure blocks) that force the customer into obsolete on-premise hardware deployments.

## Rationale
Defines the revenue ceiling vs. customer scaling capabilities. For a startup, it is vital to retain the ability to upsell as customers grow, rather than locking them into a low-value, flat-rate enterprise seat forever.

## Negotiation Tips
- Provisions that tie software to a specific MAC address or physical server, which prevents the customer from utilizing modern, scalable cloud and virtualized infrastructure.
