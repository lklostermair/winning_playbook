# Data Security Liability

## Standard Position
Liability for data security limited to direct costs of restoration, subject to the overall liability cap, and conditioned upon buyer compliance with security best practices.

## Fallback Position
- A separate, higher 'Super-Cap' specifically for data breaches, tied to the limits of the startup’s mandatory cyber-insurance policy.

## Red Line
Accepting liability for third-party hacking or cyber-breaches regardless of the security standards implemented or the buyer’s own operational negligence.

## Rationale
Essential for SaaS products. Protects against catastrophic breach damages while acknowledging the buyer's role in security.

## Negotiation Tips
- Accepting liability for 'all data loss' without requiring the buyer to follow standard backup protocols or maintain their own secure environment.
