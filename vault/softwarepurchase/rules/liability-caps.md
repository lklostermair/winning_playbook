# Liability Caps

## Standard Position
Total aggregate liability for all contractual or tort claims is capped at 100% of the fees paid in the preceding 12 months. Explicit, broad exclusion of all indirect, incidental, and consequential damages.

## Fallback Position
- A 'Super-Cap' structure (e.g., 2-3x fees) specifically reserved for high-risk areas like Data Privacy (GDPR), while keeping a lower, standard cap for general performance issues.

## Red Line
Unlimited liability for any contractual breach, which effectively creates an unquantifiable financial risk that would block any professional VC investment.

## Rationale
The #1 priority for startups. If the software causes a crash at a major client, the damage could exceed the startup's valuation. Caps are non-negotiable for VC-backed entities.

## Negotiation Tips
- Contracts that exclude liability caps for 'gross negligence' or 'breach of confidentiality'—terms that can be interpreted aggressively to circumvent total liability protections.
