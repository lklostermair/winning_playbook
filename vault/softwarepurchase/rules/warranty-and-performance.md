# Warranty and Performance

## Standard Position
12-month performance warranty against explicitly defined functional specifications. The exclusive remedy is limited to 'repair or replace' functionality to mitigate cash-intensive settlement risks.

## Fallback Position
- A 90-day warranty coupled with a robust, commercially standard Support & Maintenance SLA (Service Level Agreement) to manage customer expectations over the long term.

## Red Line
A total exclusion of all warranties (e.g., 'Sold As-Is') in a high-value B2B purchase, as this lack of quality guarantee destroys customer trust and market position.

## Rationale
Product reliability and stability define the brand’s market reputation. Startups must guarantee functionality but avoid 'perfection' clauses that are technically impossible to fulfill.

## Negotiation Tips
- Ambiguous promises like 'the software will be error-free,' which is an impossible legal standard that opens the company to endless frivolous breach-of-contract lawsuits.
