# Exceptions to Confidential Information

## Standard Position
All four standard exceptions must be included: (i) publicly available information; (ii) already in recipient's possession; (iii) received from a third party without restriction; (iv) independently developed without use of confidential information

## Fallback Position
- Accept three of four exceptions if the missing one poses low practical risk given the specific engagement
- Accept modified language (e.g., requiring written evidence of prior possession) provided all four categories are conceptually covered

## Red Line
Never accept an NDA with fewer than three standard exceptions. The "independently developed" exception is critical for diversified organizations

_Not specified._

## Escalation Logic
If the "independently developed" exception is missing and cannot be added, escalate.

## Rationale
Standard exceptions protect the receiving party from being held liable for information it already knew, independently developed, or received from other legitimate sources. Without these exceptions, a large, diversified organization could inadvertently breach the NDA simply by using knowledge it already possessed.

## Negotiation Tips
- Some NDAs omit one or more standard exceptions — particularly the "independently developed" exception. For large organizations with multiple business units conducting parallel R&D, this omission creates significant risk.
