# Permitted Recipients

## Standard Position
Need-to-know disclosure to employees, affiliates, agents, advisors, contractors with equivalent NDA

## Fallback Position
- Employees and affiliates with prior notice
- Employees and affiliates with prior consent

## Red Line
Limits disclosure exclusively to named individuals or requires consent for sharing with affiliated entities
