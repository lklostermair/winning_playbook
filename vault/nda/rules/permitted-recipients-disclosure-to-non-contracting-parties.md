# Permitted Recipients — Disclosure to Non-Contracting Parties

## Standard Position
Permitted disclosure to employees, affiliates, agents, advisors, and contractors on a need-to-know basis, provided they are bound by equivalent confidentiality obligations

## Fallback Position
- Permitted disclosure to employees and affiliates only, with prior written notice (not consent) required for external advisors and contractors
- Permitted disclosure with prior written consent, provided that consent shall not be unreasonably withheld or delayed

## Red Line
Reject any NDA that limits disclosure exclusively to named individuals or requires consent for sharing with affiliated entities

## Decision Logic
Modern business operations require sharing information with affiliates, subsidiaries, consultants, subcontractors, and advisors. If the NDA restricts disclosure only to the signatory's direct employees, it may be impossible to fulfill the Purpose without breaching the agreement. Some NDAs prohibit any disclosure to third parties, or require the disclosing party's prior written consent for each instance of sharing. This is operationally impractical, especially for organizations that rely on affiliated entities, external advisors, or contractors.

## Escalation Logic
If the NDA does not permit sharing with affiliates or advisors under any circumstances, escalate.
