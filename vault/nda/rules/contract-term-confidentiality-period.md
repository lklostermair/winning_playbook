# Contract Term / Confidentiality Period

## Standard Position
NDA term: 2–3 years. Confidentiality: 5 years from each disclosure, surviving termination

## Fallback Position
- Confidentiality period 3–7 years from disclosure
- Confidentiality = NDA term + fixed survival (e.g., 3 years post-termination)

## Red Line
Perpetual/indefinite confidentiality

## Decision Logic
_Not specified._

## Escalation Logic
Counterparty insists on perpetual confidentiality or a confidentiality period shorter than 3 years

## Rationale
Ensures manageable, defined obligations
