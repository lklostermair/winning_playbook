# Marking of Confidential Info

## Standard Position
Must be marked "Confidential"

## Fallback Position
- Information is confidential if marked OR if its confidential nature would be evident to a reasonable person
- All information is deemed confidential, but with a practical mechanism for the disclosing party to designate specific items as non-confidential

## Red Line
Definition that treats all information as confidential with no marking mechanism and no reasonable-person standard

## Decision Logic
_Not specified._

## Escalation Logic
Counterparty insists that all information is confidential with no exceptions or marking process

## Rationale
Controls scope of what's protected and compliance burden
